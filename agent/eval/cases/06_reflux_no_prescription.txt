Doctor: Tell me what is bothering you.
Patient: I get a burning feeling in my chest after dinner, especially when I lie down.
Doctor: How long has this been happening?
Patient: About a month.
Doctor: Any difficulty swallowing or weight loss?
Patient: No.
Doctor: This sounds like acid reflux. Some people need omeprazole for this, but in your case I don't think you need any medicine right now.
Doctor: Eat dinner at least three hours before sleeping, avoid spicy and oily food, and raise the head of your bed.
Doctor: If it does not improve in four weeks, come back and we will discuss medicine.
Patient: Okay, I will try that.
