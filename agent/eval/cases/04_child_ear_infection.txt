Doctor: What happened to the little one?

Doctor: Any rash or vomiting?
Mother: No rash, no vomiting.
Doctor: Is he allergic to any medicine?

Doctor: His temperature is 102. The right eardrum is red and bulging. He has a middle ear infection.
Doctor: Give him Augmentin suspension 312 mg, 5 ml twice a day for seven days.
